## 主 Prompt（英文，用于 AI 图像生成）

```
A perfectly round, sphere-shaped ink drop floating suspended in clear water, viewed from the front. No pointed tip, no teardrop shape — purely spherical like a ball of liquid ink hovering in water. The ink inside the sphere is not uniform: a large off-center patch of deep solid black ink sits slightly to the upper-left area inside the sphere, as if the dense ink has shifted like a heavy liquid center of gravity, while the rest of the sphere is a lighter translucent gray with visible ink wash gradients. This creates the impression that the ink inside is sloshing and settling.

The sphere is slowly rotating, and from its surface, extremely thin and delicate ink filaments spiral outward into the surrounding water — like ink trails being flung off by the rotation. These ink wisps curve in gentle arcs around the sphere, following the rotation direction, forming loose spiral trails that gradually dissipate and fade into the water. The wisps are hair-thin, elegant, and flowing — like smoke curls or silk threads caught in a gentle whirlpool. They wrap loosely around the sphere at various distances, some close to the surface, some extending further out before fading to nothing.

Two clean circular white voids on the sphere serve as eyes — pure white holes where no ink exists, revealing the light behind the sphere.

The overall feeling: a living ink sphere gently spinning in still water, ink slowly bleeding out in beautiful spiral trails. Meditative, elegant, like a high-speed photograph of ink dropping into water. NOT a lotus, NOT a flower, NOT organic tentacles. Pure suspended ink dynamics. Transparent background PNG.

CRITICAL COLOR RULE: All ink MUST be strictly monochrome grayscale — pure black (#000000) to light gray (#CCCCCC). ZERO warm tones, ZERO brown, ZERO sepia, ZERO amber, ZERO beige. The sphere body and wisps must have NO color cast whatsoever — mathematically neutral R=G=B values only.
```

## 中文翻译版（便于理解与修改）

```
一枚完美正圆形的墨滴球悬浮于清水中，从正面观察。无尖头，无泪滴形——纯粹的球形，像一颗液态墨球悬停在水中。

球体内的墨色并不均匀：左上区域有一大块偏浓的实心黑墨，像重液的重心发生了偏移；而球体其余部分是较浅的半透明灰色，带有明显的水墨晕染渐变。这给人一种球内的墨正在晃动和沉淀的感觉。

球体正在缓慢旋转，从球表面向四周的水中散发极纤细的墨丝——像是旋转时被甩出的墨尾。这些墨丝围绕球体划出柔缓的弧线，跟随旋转方向，形成松散的螺旋尾迹，逐渐消散并融入水中。墨丝如发丝般纤细、优雅、流动——像被轻柔漩涡卷住的烟丝或丝线。它们以不同距离松散地环绕球体，一些贴近表面，一些延伸较远后彻底消失。

球体上有两个干净的圆形白色空洞作为眼睛——这是两个不存在墨水的纯白圆孔，透出球体后方的光线。

整体感觉：一个活着的墨球在静水中轻轻旋转，墨水以优美的螺旋轨迹缓慢渗出。静谧、优雅，像高速摄影捕捉到的墨滴滴入水中的瞬间。不是荷花，不是花朵，不是有机触手。纯粹的悬浮墨动力学。透明背景 PNG。
```

## 负面关键词（避免）

```
NOT a lotus, NOT a flower, NOT organic tentacles, NOT teardrop shape, NOT pointed tip, NOT cartoon, NOT anime, NOT 3D render, NOT metallic, NOT brown, NOT sepia, NOT amber, NOT wood-colored, NOT coffee-colored, NOT warm-toned, not beige, not tan
不是荷花，不是花朵，不是有机触手，不是泪滴形，不是尖头，不是卡通，不是动漫，不是3D渲染，不是金属，不是棕色，不是咖啡色，不是琥珀色，不是木色，不是暖色调，不是米色
```

> ⚠️ **颜色注意**：AI 写作教练的 UI 使用了木质色设计规范（`#d4c4b0` / `#8b7355` / `#4a3820`），这些颜色可能作为上下文泄露到图像生成中，导致墨水偏向咖啡色。所有生成素材必须严格保持**纯灰度**（`#000` ~ `#ccc`），零暖色偏移。

## 变体方向建议

| 变体 | 调整参数 | 用途 |
|------|----------|------|
| **更浓墨** | 增加 deep solid black 比例，减少 translucent gray | 专注/高强度状态 |
| **更淡墨** | 增加 lighter gray 比例，减少 black patch | 空闲/休息状态 |
| **加速旋转** | faster rotation / more dynamic spiral trails | 交互反馈动画 |
| **更小眼睛** | eyes smaller / closer together | 更专注的表情 |
| **墨丝更密** | more ink filaments / denser wisps | 强调"呼吸"感 |

## 实现建议（供未来动画参考）

1. **多帧静态图**：生成 5-8 帧不同旋转角度的静态 PNG，通过 CSS/Canvas 循环播放实现自转动画
2. **墨丝粒子层**：在球体图像之上叠加 Canvas 粒子层，模拟墨丝的生成、旋转和消散
3. **颜色混合模式**：使用 `mix-blend-mode: multiply` 或 `darken` 让墨色与背景产生自然的水墨渗透效果
